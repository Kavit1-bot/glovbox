Glovbox

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 24, 2026

Glovbox Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") operates www.glovbox.net. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you register for Glovbox, we collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Vehicle registration numbers
  • Contact information (if provided)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We collect:

  • Browser type and version
  • IP address
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Device information

1.3 Vehicle Information

When you add a vehicle, we retrieve publicly available information from the UK DVLA database including:

  • Make and model
  • MOT expiry date
  • Road tax status
  • Vehicle colour and year

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide vehicle tracking and reminder services
  • Send MOT and road tax expiry reminders
  • Improve our service
  • Communicate service updates
  • Provide customer support

3. Sharing Your Information

3.1 Service Partners

When you use our comparison services, we may share relevant information with:

  • Insurance comparison partners (with your consent)
  • MOT centre booking partners (with your consent)
  • Breakdown cover providers (with your consent)

3.2 We Will Never

  • Sell your personal data to third parties
  • Share your data without your explicit consent
  • Send spam or unsolicited marketing

4. Email Communications

We send:

  • Service emails: MOT and tax reminders (these are essential to our service)
  • Marketing emails: Special offers from partners (you can opt out anytime)

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email.

5. Data Security

We protect your data using:

  • 256-bit SSL encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure password hashing (bcrypt)
  • Regular security audits
  • Secure server infrastructure

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing

To exercise any of these rights, email us at: privacy@glovbox.net

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze site usage (anonymized)

See our Cookie Policy for full details.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data:

  • Account data: Until you delete your account
  • Vehicle data: Until you remove the vehicle
  • Email logs: 30 days
  • Analytics data: 12 months (anonymized)

9. Third-Party Services

We use:

  • DVLA API: To retrieve vehicle information
  • Brevo: Email delivery service
  • Railway: Hosting infrastructure
  • Google Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

10. Children's Privacy

Glovbox is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data is processed and stored in the UK and EU. If we transfer data outside the EEA, we ensure adequate safeguards are in place.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or a prominent notice on our website.
