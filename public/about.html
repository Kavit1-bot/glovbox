About Glovbox

Helping UK drivers never miss MOT or road tax deadlines

Our Story

Glovbox was founded in 2025 with a simple mission: help UK drivers avoid unnecessary late fees and penalties.

Every year, thousands of UK drivers pay £80 late fees for expired MOTs or road tax because they simply forget the deadline. We knew there had to be a better way.

That's why we built Glovbox - a free, simple platform that automatically reminds you when your MOT or road tax is expiring. No more calendar reminders, no more sticky notes on the dashboard, just automated email alerts at exactly the right time.

Our Mission

To make vehicle management effortless for every UK driver. We believe no one should pay late fees or penalties simply because they forgot a deadline.

What We Do

🔔 Automated Reminders

We send email reminders 30, 14, and 7 days before your MOT or road tax expires. Never miss a deadline again.

🔧 MOT Centre Finder

Find trusted MOT centres near you with real distances, opening hours, and contact details. Book your MOT in minutes.

💰 Insurance Comparison

Compare quotes from leading UK insurers and save up to £300 per year on your car insurance.

📊 Vehicle Dashboard

Track unlimited vehicles in one place. Perfect for families or small businesses with multiple cars.

How We Make Money

Glovbox is free for users. We earn small commissions when you use our partner services:

  • MOT centre bookings
  • Insurance comparisons
  • Breakdown cover

This never affects the price you pay. Our partners pay us a referral fee, and we pass the savings on to you.

We're transparent about our partnerships and always put your best interests first.

Official Data Sources

All vehicle information on Glovbox comes directly from:

🇬🇧 UK Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency (DVLA)

We use the official DVLA API to retrieve accurate MOT expiry dates, road tax status, and vehicle details. This ensures 100% accuracy and compliance with UK regulations.

Glovbox is not affiliated with or endorsed by the DVLA, but we use their official data to provide you with accurate information.
