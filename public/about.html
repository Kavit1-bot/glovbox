About Glovbox

Glovbox is a vehicle management platform designed to help UK drivers never miss another MOT or road tax deadline.

Our Mission

We believe managing your vehicle shouldn't be complicated. Our mission is to make it effortless for UK drivers to:

  • Track MOT and road tax expiry dates
  • Get timely reminders before deadlines
  • Find trusted MOT centres nearby
  • Compare insurance quotes and save money
  • Avoid late fees and penalties

What We Do

Free Vehicle Checks

We provide unlimited free vehicle checks using official DVLA data. Enter any UK registration number to see MOT status, tax status, and expiry dates instantly.

Automated Reminders

Sign up to receive email and SMS reminders 30, 14, and 7 days before your MOT or road tax expires. Never pay a late fee again.

MOT Centre Finder

Find approved MOT test centres near you with real distances, opening hours, ratings, and phone numbers. Call to book directly.

Insurance Comparison

Get free estimated insurance quotes based on your vehicle and driver profile. Compare prices from leading UK insurers and could save up to £150 per year.

Multi-Vehicle Dashboard

Track unlimited vehicles in one place. Perfect for families with multiple cars or small businesses managing a fleet.

How It Works

1. Check Your Vehicle - Free instant lookup using DVLA records
2. Sign Up - Create a free account (30-day trial, no credit card)
3. Add Vehicles - Save your vehicle registrations to your dashboard
4. Set Reminders - Choose email, SMS, or both
5. Relax - We'll remind you before any deadline

Data Sources

We use official and trusted data sources:

  • DVLA (Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency): Vehicle MOT and tax information
  • Google Maps: MOT centre locations, ratings, and contact details
  • Industry-Standard Calculations: Insurance quote estimates
