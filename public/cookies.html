Cookie Policy

Last Updated: February 19, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Glovbox uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to site owners.

Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Understand how you use our service
  • Improve our website performance
  • Provide relevant content and features

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Required for site functionality (login, security) Session / 7 days
Functional Remember preferences and settings 30 days - 1 year
Analytics Understand usage patterns and improve service 2 years
Authentication Keep you logged in securely 7 days

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off:

  • glovbox_token: Authentication token to keep you signed in
  • glovbox_user: Basic user information for personalization
  • glovbox_vehicles: Saved vehicle registrations (stored locally)

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization:

  • Language preferences
  • Region/location settings
  • Dashboard customization preferences

Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use our site:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Features used most frequently
  • User journey and navigation patterns
  • Device type and browser information
Note: We do not use advertising or tracking cookies from third-party ad networks.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services we use:

  • Stripe: Payment processing (essential for transactions)
  • Google Maps: MOT centre search and location services

These services have their own cookie policies which we do not control.

How to Control Cookies

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy → Cookies

Clearing Cookies

You can delete all cookies from your browser. However, this will:

  • Sign you out of Glovbox
  • Remove your saved preferences
  • Require re-authentication

Blocking Cookies

You can set your browser to block cookies. However:

  • Essential features may not work
  • You won't be able to sign in
  • Saved vehicles won't persist

Local Storage

We also use browser local storage for:

  • Authentication tokens
  • User preferences
  • Saved vehicle information

This data is stored on your device and not sent to our servers automatically.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
