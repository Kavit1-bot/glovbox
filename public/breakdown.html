💡 Tips for Choosing Breakdown Cover

  • ✓ Consider how much you drive - more miles = more likely to break down
  • ✓ Older cars (10+ years) benefit more from complete cover
  • ✓ Check if your car insurance includes breakdown cover already
  • ✓ European cover is essential if you drive abroad
  • ✓ Home start is worth it if you have an older battery
