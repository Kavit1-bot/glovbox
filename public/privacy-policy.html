Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Introduction

Glovbox ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our vehicle management platform.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name and email address
  • Vehicle registration numbers and details
  • Service history and maintenance records
  • Payment information (if applicable)

Automatically Collected Information

  • Browser type and version
  • IP address
  • Usage data and analytics

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To send MOT and tax expiry reminders
  • To manage your account
  • To improve our platform
  • To communicate with you about updates and offers

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes:

  • Encryption of passwords using bcrypt
  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Regular security assessments
  • Restricted access to personal data

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the UK or European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request copies of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request transfer of your data
  • Right to Object: You can object to our processing of your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from your Account Settings page. This will permanently remove all your personal data, vehicles, and service records from our systems. This action cannot be undone.

Cookies

We use session storage to maintain your login state. This data is automatically deleted when you close your browser. We do not use tracking cookies or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • DVLA API: To retrieve vehicle information
  • Google Maps API: To find nearby MOT centres
  • Brevo: To send email reminders

These services have their own privacy policies and we recommend reviewing them.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete all your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain certain information.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
